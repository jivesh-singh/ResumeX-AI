Core Capabilities

Engineered to Beat Modern Applicant Tracking Systems

Every feature is purposefully built to address exact recruiter filtering criteria, parser traps, and high-impact interview qualifications.

Precision ATS Scoring

Simulates enterprise parser algorithms (Workday, Greenhouse, Taleo) to score your document structure, section clarity, contact extraction, and syntax compatibility.

  • Section hierarchy validation
  • Multi-column layout risk detection
  • Contact & date parse confirmation

Diagnostic AI Feedback

Actionable line-by-line feedback targeting weak verbs, missing business outcomes, and passive descriptions to elevate your resume into the top percentile.

  • Action-verb impact enhancement
  • Numerical metrics quantification
  • Repetition and cliché mitigation

Target Keyword Matching

Extracts high-priority hard skills, industry frameworks, and certifications from your target job description and cross-references them against your resume content.

  • Exact & semantic keyword overlap
  • Keyword density measurement
  • Contextual placement review

Skill Gap Analysis

Pinpoints critical competencies and certifications explicitly demanded by employers that are currently missing from your resume profile.

  • Categorized missing skill tags
  • One-click suggested phrasing
  • Domain-specific tool taxonomy

Career Trajectory Advisory

Translates your skill matrix into upward career moves, providing role progression maps, compensation benchmark insights, and strategic interview focus areas.

  • Next-level title alignment
  • Core competency upskilling roadmap
  • Tailored interview talking points
